Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Daily Infinity

Short version: your habits stay on your phone, there is no account, and optional analytics never see what your habits are called.

What stays on your phone

Your habits, notes, check-ins and settings are stored only on your device. Daily Infinity does not receive them and cannot see them. There is no account or sign-up.

Optional analytics and crash reports

The first time you open the app it asks whether to help improve GraceDay. Until you say yes, no analytics or crash data is collected. If you allow it, GraceDay uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect:

  • App interactions, such as which screens you open and that a check-in happened;
  • Crash logs and diagnostics when the app fails;
  • Device and instance identifiers (a random app-instance ID, device model, Android version).

This never includes habit names, notes or any text you type. Firebase acts as a service provider for us; the data is sent encrypted. You can change your choice at any time in You → Help improve GraceDay.

Export and delete

In You → Backup you can export your data to a file or import it again. You → Delete all my data erases everything on your phone. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email dailyinfinity.support@gmail.com.

What we do not do

No ads, no selling or sharing of data, no tracking across other apps.

Children

GraceDay is meant for people 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes.
