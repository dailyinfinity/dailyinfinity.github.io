Delete your data

GraceDay by Daily Infinity. There is no account and no server copy of your habits, so deleting is one step in the app.

In the app

  1. Open GraceDay and tap You.
  2. Tap Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm twice.

All your habits, check-ins and settings are deleted from your phone immediately. GraceDay also resets its analytics data, turns analytics and crash reports off, and returns to the start.

What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

DataWhereWhat happens
Habits, check-ins, grace days, hearts, reminders, pauses, name, settingsYour phone onlyDeleted at once by Delete all my data (or by uninstalling the app)
Backup files you exportedWherever you saved themYours to delete; we never receive them
Anonymous analytics (only if you agreed)Google FirebaseDeleted automatically after 2 months
Anonymous crash reports (only if you agreed)Google FirebaseDeleted automatically after 90 days

Ask for earlier deletion

To have the anonymous Firebase data deleted before those periods end, email dailyinfinity.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data. We answer within 30 days.
