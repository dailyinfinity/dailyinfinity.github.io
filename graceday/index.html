How GraceDay forgives

Four safety nets, so a rough day stays a rough day.

You never have to remember to use them. They work on their own, and every one is visible in the app so you always know where you stand.

  • Grace days

    Miss a day and a grace day covers it, automatically. Your streak carries on and the day shows as a gold leaf. It works like a streak freeze, except it is built in and you never buy it.

    Free: 1 a month

  • Two hearts

    Out of grace days? A miss costs a heart instead. Show up the next day and you win the heart back.

    Always on

  • Rest days

    Choose the days a habit is not expected, like weekends. They never count against you, and they never break a run.

    Free

  • Pause

    Sick, travelling, or deep in exams? Pause a habit and it waits right where you left it, with its history intact.

    Pro

Honest by design: two misses in a row still end a run, so a streak always means something.

Pause

Sick? Travelling? Pause, don't break.

Pick the habits, say why, set the dates. While a habit is paused its streak freezes, nothing is lost, and it picks up on the day you come back.

  • Pause one habit or several
  • Up to 30 days at a time
  • Reminders go quiet until you return

Questions

Good to know before you install.

Does a missed day really not break my streak?

If you have a grace day, it covers the missed day on its own and the streak continues. If you do not, a heart is used. If you miss two days in a row and both safety nets are empty, the run ends. Your best run and your history are always kept.

Is GraceDay free?

Yes. The free version has no ads and no account, and gives you up to 3 habits, a grace day every month, two hearts, rest days, reminders and the widget. GraceDay Pro is an optional subscription for unlimited habits, weekly grace days, full history, pause, insights and cloud sync.

Do I need to make an account?

No. GraceDay works fully without one. An account is only for cloud sync, if you want your habits on more than one phone.

How do I move to a new phone?

Open You → Cloud sync on the old phone and sign in. On the new phone, choose I already use GraceDay and sign in with the same account. Loading your data on a new phone is always free.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. GraceDay is on Android 7.0 and later for now.

How do I delete my data?

Open You → Delete all my data to wipe everything from your phone, or You → Cloud sync → Delete account to remove your account and synced copy. Here is exactly how deletion works.
