Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to dailyinfinity.support@gmail.com. We read every message.

Move to a new phone

Open You → Cloud sync and sign in. Your habits sync to your account automatically. On a new phone, sign in with the same account (from the first screen, I already use GraceDay, or from You → Cloud sync) and your data loads by itself. Loading is always free; keeping the cloud copy up to date is a Pro feature.

Delete your data

Open You → Delete all my data. Everything is removed from your phone at once. See how deletion works.

Reminders not arriving?

Check that notifications are allowed for GraceDay in your phone settings, and that battery saver is not restricting the app. Reminders are re-planned each time you open the app.

Include in your message

Your phone model, Android version and what you saw. Please do not send habit names or notes unless you want to.
