Delete your data

See also the privacy policy.

GraceDay by DailyInfinity Studios. Your habits live on your phone. If you also use the optional cloud sync, there is an account and a server copy, and you can delete those in the app too.

In the app

  1. Open GraceDay and tap You.
  2. Tap Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm twice.

All your habits, check-ins and settings are deleted from your phone immediately. GraceDay also resets its analytics data, turns analytics and crash reports off, and returns to the start.

Delete your account and synced data

  1. Open GraceDay, tap You, then Cloud sync.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice.

Your account and your synced data are deleted at once, and you are signed out. Your habits on this phone are not touched. Delete all my data clears this phone only.

If you can no longer open the app, email dailyinfinity.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account, and we delete it within 30 days.
